# Fact-Check Notes

## Certainty levels

- **Primary-direct:** Original paper, artifact, post, or statement.
- **Documented correspondence:** Preserved messages with a provenance chain.
- **Contemporaneous report:** Reporting close to the event.
- **Participant retrospective:** Firsthand account recorded later.
- **Widely accepted interpretation:** Synthesis supported by several records.
- **Plausible inference:** Reasonable but not directly documented.
- **Speculative or disputed:** Qualify clearly or omit.
